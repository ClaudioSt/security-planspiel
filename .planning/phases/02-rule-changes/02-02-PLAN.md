<objective>
Make RoS count only the team's own security spend (RULE-06, D-21, D-12). Today `evaluate_plan` passes the budget total, which includes event OPEX (the Operative-Unterstützung discount lowers it, penalties would raise it), into `ros()`. Split the ledger into a security-spend total for RoS and the budget total for the over-budget check and the Budget Review, and route every RoS consumer through own spend.

Purpose: RoS should measure the efficiency of the team's own security decisions, symmetric for penalties and discounts (PROJECT.md decision 2026-09-29).
Output: `security_spend` and a two-total `BudgetLedger` in `planspiel/rules.py`; consumers in enumerate/report/analyze updated; property tests; a reviewed RoS snapshot change (characterization goldens only; digests do not contain RoS and `costs.total` keeps its budget meaning); README/CLAUDE.md/PROJECT.md updated.
</objective>

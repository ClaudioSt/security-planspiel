A further strict test pins the interim meaning of the committed {1, 1} weights (k = 1 under the new formula), so the interim state is deliberate and visible.

Purpose: RoS·100 no longer dominates KZ once tuning sets k; the debrief winner metric becomes hand-computable (D-14).
Output: planspiel/index_weights.py, new rules.final_index, filter removal, adapted tests and docs.
</objective>

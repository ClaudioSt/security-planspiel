<objective>
Prove that the printed hand-calculation steps give the same results as the simulation (PRINT-04, ROADMAP SC3). A replay in `tests/support/` encodes the worksheet steps a second time, reading only the `AttackSheet` from plan 04-02, and is compared with `rules.apply_attack` and `rules.wave_kz_change` over the dense synthetic grid of D-12 (fast) and over every enumerated plan (slow). A key guard and negative controls make the test fail whenever the simulation applies a mechanic the sheet does not carry (D-11, D-21). A brute force confirms the two-decimal hint (D-09).

Purpose: facilitators calculate by hand from these sheets; this plan shows that following the printed steps can never disagree with the simulation, before plan 04-06 commits the PDFs.
Output: replay helpers, fast equivalence tests, slow full replay.

Rules for this plan: the replay is the one deliberate second encoding (research "Don't Hand-Roll"); it must not import or call `planspiel.rules`/`planspiel.rounding`, and its function names must not repeat any name in `FORMULA_FUNCTIONS` of `tests/test_single_definition.py` (no `apply_attack`, `wave_kz_change`, `round_half_away`, …). If a comparison fails, the model or the rules are wrong, not the replay: fix `planspiel/printmodel.py` test-first in a separate commit, explain it in the SUMMARY, and never bend the replay towards the simulation.

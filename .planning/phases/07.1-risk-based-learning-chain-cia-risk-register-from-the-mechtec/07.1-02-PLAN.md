<objective>
RISK-02: open the Teamheft with a risk register page: five risks R1–R5 with asset, threat plus source, C/I/A, business-impact category plus sentence, rank, treat/accept plus reason and a Rückblick column; a 3×3 likelihood × impact matrix with colour zones and the config € bands on the impact axis; a short instruction and one filled-in example from another industry. The page passes the veil, digit and fill checks.

Purpose: the core learning goal (CONTEXT domain, priority 1): teams derive risks from the company profile and set measures on that basis, using the CIA triad. Today the threat model is only implicit in the story documents; the register makes the chain explicit on paper before any measure is chosen.

Output: `RegisterPage` model, `teamheft_register.tex.j2`, zone colours, register texts, README and Was-ist-neu wording, tests.

Decisions honoured: D-01 (rows from `register_rows`), D-02 (columns, short headers, categories production / customer (OEM) / know-how / costs at Claude's discretion), D-03 (3×3 matrix, colour zones, rank column), D-04 (€ bands on the impact axis, composed from config), D-07 (instruction plus clinic example), D-08 (five pages, register first), D-11 (treat/accept tick plus reason), D-13 (no re-ranking step; the Rückblick replaces it), D-20 and D-25 (Rückblick reference and the not-hit rule), D-21 (Rückblick filled in the closing block), D-23 (README and Was-ist-neu parts of this plan). Layout choice (discretion): portrait, table stacked above the matrix, matrix left with the Rückblick note right; RESEARCH § RISK-02 proved 669.6 of 731.2 pt for this arrangement.

Hard rules: `uv run` only; numbers only from config (R-labels, rank range and € ranges are composed in code with `fmt.format_number` / `fmt.format_money`); prose only in `content/texte.toml`, count-free and without number words; templates only loop and place; the committed print set in `final_documents/generated/` is not touched (RISK-10 writes it).
</objective>

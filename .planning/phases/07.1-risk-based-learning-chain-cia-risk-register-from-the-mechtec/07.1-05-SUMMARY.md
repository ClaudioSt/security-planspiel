## Details

- Config: `risk_matrix.cases` holds ids and bands only: production_day medium, production_days high (ransomware), production_week high (ot), design_data medium (exfil), oem_hold high, audit_findings low, single_pc low. No euro amount per case exists anywhere.
- Validator: `check_risk_cases` reuses `risk.band_of` over the already rebuilt `RiskBand` tuple; only an anchored case is compared with `base_severity x s_unit` (raw dict subscripts). Product rules (6 to 8 cases, every wave attack anchored, every band used) are plain asserts in `tests/test_risk_matrix_config.py`, as is `test_only_anchored_cases_are_tied_to_the_attack_damage`.
- Texts: `[sheets.cheatsheet]` plus seven `[sheets.cheatsheet.cases.<id>]`; digit-free, no number words (`sheets.cheatsheet` added to `PHASE7_PREFIXES`); `required_keys` demands them only when cases exist. The production_day answer says "Arbeitstag" on purpose (annual revenue over working days is about 60,000 EUR, band medium).
- Veil: model test runs every fragment through `PROFILE_STORY` and `PROFILE_NAMES`; `CheatCase` has no `like_attack` field and `cheatsheet.py` never mentions it.
- Story direction (`sheets.ablauf.blocks.story_groups.direction`) now points to the sheet by its title.
- Was-ist-neu: `sheets.was_neu.material.clues` reworded to "Neu im Spielleitungsheft: Antworten zu Geschäftsfolgen und die" (62 characters, was 64); no row added; `tests/test_was_neu_pdf.py` still passes (8 passed).
- README: § Spielablauf step 2, § Spielmaterial (`spielleitung-uebersicht.pdf` bullet), § Parametrisierung.
- Page fill (log line of the built booklet): the cheat page is report page 4 with `used=351.24513pt text=731.23584pt`, about 48 percent of the text height; the print-list page before it is page 3 with 354.8 pt. No Overfull line, no missing glyph.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] case anchor check cascaded into unrelated validator tests**
- **Found during:** full fast suite after Task 3
- **Issue:** `risk.case_attack` was defined as "attack used in waves". `tests/test_validate.py` deliberately breaks a wave's `attack_id`, which un-used the `ot` attack and added a `risk.case_attack` error beside the expected reference errors (2 failures).
- **Fix:** an anchor is valid when it is an attack defined in `attacks`; consistency compares against the defined attack's damage. Added `_Validator.defined_unprotected_damages`; `used_unprotected_damages` now filters from it. Test renamed to `test_a_like_attack_must_be_an_attack_of_the_config`.
- **Commit:** 733fe70

- **Fix:** the test-only tie config drops `risk_matrix.cases` (the rounding rule it tests does not involve them).
- **Commit:** 733fe70

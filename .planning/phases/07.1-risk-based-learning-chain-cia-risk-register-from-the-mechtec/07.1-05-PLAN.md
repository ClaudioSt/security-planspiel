RISK-05: give the facilitator booklet a business-impact cheat sheet of 6–8 business cases phrased as consequences, never as attacks, each with an answer and a band from config; the facilitator answers team questions in the role of MechTech's management with a € order of magnitude that agrees with the game's damage values.

Purpose: business impact married to the risk assessment (learning priority 3) and the user's chosen communication practice (D-05): teams ask "what would that cost us?", the facilitator answers as management with a band. D-22 makes the answer consistent with the later worksheet.

Output: `risk_matrix.cases` in config with loader and validator, `planspiel/cheatsheet.py`, cheat texts, a new booklet page, story-direction sentence, README lines, tests.

Decisions honoured: D-05 (role of management, € order of magnitude from the bands, story direction), D-06 (6–8 cases, consequences not attacks, figures from config, prose from texte.toml), D-22 as clarified on 2026-11-13 (user choice "Nur für Angriffs-Fälle": the band equality applies only to cases anchored to an attack with `like_attack`, checked by the validator; unanchored cases are plausibility judgements from the MechTech profile; every wave attack has at least one anchored case; no further anchoring), D-23 (README parts; the Was-ist-neu clue line is reworded within its current length to also name the business-consequence answers, because the sheet's one-page proof has only about 10 pt slack and no row may be added). Case set (discretion, RESEARCH A3/A4): seven cases, two low, two medium, three high, with `like_attack` anchors ransomware -> high, ot -> high, exfil -> medium; the four unanchored cases stay unanchored.

Hard rules: `uv run` only; numbers and ids only in config, prose only in texte.toml (digit-free, no number words); no exact euro amount per case (it could equal a hidden damage); templates only place; the committed print set is not touched.
</objective>

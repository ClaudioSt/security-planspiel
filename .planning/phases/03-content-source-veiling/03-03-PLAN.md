<objective>
Prove that every attack is inferable from at least two clues in the pre-wave material (VEIL-03, ROADMAP SC4) and veil the story documents so that the clues remain but the names are gone (D-16, D-21). A data file `content/clues.toml` holds the clue matrix with verbatim quotes (D-17); `planspiel clues` prints the facilitator view and fails on fewer than two clues, fewer than two distinct documents (D-18), a quote not found verbatim, or a non-pre-wave source. The story giveaway check (story profile with the D-20 exceptions) goes RED on the known hits first, then the drafted rewrites turn it GREEN. The user reviews the rewrites and the matrix at a blocking checkpoint before they are committed (D-13d).

Purpose: players should work out the coming attack from the company profile, the newspaper and the mails, never read its name; facilitators get a checked matrix of where each clue sits.
Output: `content/clues.toml`, `Clue`, `load_clues`, `check_clues`, `FacilitatorView`, `format_clue_matrix`, `planspiel clues`, story scope of `check_giveaway`, `PreAttackView.story`, veiled story texts.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| story content → players before a wave | Pre-wave documents must not name an attack |
| clue file → facilitator material | The matrix names attacks and must stay facilitator-only |
| story editor → clue quotes | Later story edits can silently break clues |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-10 | Information disclosure | story texts naming an attack or a technique term | high | mitigate | Story-scope giveaway check in `planspiel texts` and the quick suite; leak test over `PreAttackView.strings()` including the story |
| T-03-11 | Tampering | clue quote no longer present after a story edit | medium | mitigate | Verbatim quote check after NFC without case folding (`clues.quote_not_found`) in `planspiel clues` and the quick suite |
| T-03-12 | Repudiation | attack not inferable (too few or single-source clues) | medium | mitigate | `clues.too_few` and `clues.single_source` derived from `cfg.waves` (D-18) |
| T-03-13 | Information disclosure | clue matrix (names attacks) leaking into pre-wave material | medium | mitigate | Clues stay out of `Content` and `PreAttackView`; rendered only as facilitator material in Phase 5 (PRINT-10); Phase 5 VEIL-02 scans rendered pre-wave files |
| T-03-14 | Repudiation | story rewrites committed without review | low | mitigate | Blocking decision checkpoint before commit (D-13d) |
</threat_model>

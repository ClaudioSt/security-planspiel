# Phase 07.2 Plan 01: Asset rows, case goals and matrix cleanup Summary

The `risk_matrix` config block now carries `asset_rows` (4) and a validated protection goal per cheat-sheet case, the ransomware anchor sits on a new integrity case, and all likelihood and zone code, words and colours are gone.

## What was built

- **Config (PROT-01, D-02, D-11, D-12):** `asset_rows: 4` next to `register_rows`; every case has `goal`; eight cases ordered by goal. Nothing outside `risk_matrix` changed (verified against `f631752`: only key `risk_matrix` differs).
- **Loader:** `RiskMatrix.asset_rows`, `RiskCase.goal`.

Ransomware ties C and I, ot only A, exfil only C.
- **Texts:** new `[sheets.cheatsheet.cases.data_trust]` (a business consequence, no attack named, no digits); `likelihood`, `zones`, `axis_likelihood` and `example.cell` removed from `texte.toml` and from `_REGISTER_KEYS` / `REGISTER_GROUPS`.
- **Removal (D-14):** `risk.zone_of`, `risk.ZONES`, `MatrixCell`, `MatrixRow`, `RegisterPage.matrix_rows/axis_likelihood/example_cell`, the three `risk*` colours in `planspiel.sty`. `band_of` and `band_ranges` stay. - **Deck bridge:** strict xfail on the two risk-slide vocabulary tests until 07.2-05 edits slide 32 (fast suite: 2 xfailed).
- **README:** the `risk_matrix` bullet in § Parametrisierung describes protection need, bands and cases with band and goal.

## Anchor move and rationale per case goal (for the RC4 approval, see 07.2-06)

Anchor move: the ransomware anchor was on `production_days` ("Die Produktion steht mehrere Tage still."), an availability consequence, but ransomware's strongest goals are C and I (c -2, i -2, a -1). Tagging it C or I would teach a wrong CIA mapping and tagging it A would violate D-11. So the anchor moved to the new integrity case `data_trust`; `production_days` stays band high, goal a, without anchor. The `like_attack` link is config data only and never changes the printed phrasing (business consequence, never an attack).

| Case | Band | Goal | Why mainly this loss |
|------|------|------|----------------------|
| design_data | medium | c | design data reaching a competitor is a disclosure |
| audit_findings | low | c | audit findings concern handling of protected information |
| data_trust | high | i | data that can no longer be trusted is a loss of correctness |
| production_day | medium | a | a stopped day is a loss of availability |
| production_days | high | a | several days of stoppage, availability |
| production_week | high | a | a plant out for a week, availability (matches ot's strongest goal) |
| oem_hold | high | a | orders put on ice because delivery cannot be relied on, availability of supply |
| single_pc | low | a | a machine out of service for a short time, availability |

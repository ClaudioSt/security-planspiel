# Phase 07.2 Plan 04: README flow, story direction and debriefing Summary

The facilitator booklet and the rulebook now describe the protection-need table: teams fill it before the register, questions come per asset and protection goal, the debriefing asks for the right main goal, and a sweep test keeps the 07.1 matrix vocabulary out of README and every text.

## What was built

- **Story direction** (`sheets.ablauf.blocks.story_groups.direction`): teams fill in first the protection need per asset, then the risk register; questions are asked per asset and protection goal; the cheat-sheet title is kept verbatim.
- **Debriefing** (`sheets.debrief.stages.analysis.questions`, third question): starts from the protection-need table, compares it with the wave weighting for C, I and A and asks whether the team had the right main goal per risk (D-10: discussed, not graded). `sheets.debrief.intro` now asks teams to bring the protection need and the filled register with the Rückblick. The stage entry counts did not change.

- **Tests:** `RETIRED_MATRIX_PATTERNS` with `test_the_readme_names_no_retired_matrix_term` and `test_no_text_names_a_retired_matrix_term` (12 parametrized cases), `REQUIRED_TERMS` plus Schutzbedarf and Maximumprinzip, the extended debrief and schedule assertions, and `test_the_teamheft_line_names_the_protection_need`.

# Phase 07.2 Plan 03: Protection goal on the facilitator cheat sheet Summary

Every case on the facilitator cheat sheet now prints its protection goal (C, I or A) in its own column next to the case, and the intro tells the facilitator that teams ask per asset and goal and are answered with the band of the matching case. The sheet is still one page between the print list and the first wave.

## What was built

- **Model (`planspiel/cheatsheet.py`):** `CheatCase.goal` (letter from `sheets.register.goals` via the config goal), `CheatSheetPage.goal_header` (from `sheets.cheatsheet.head_goal`). `header` stays the 3-tuple case, answer, band. Module docstring names PROT-03 and D-11.
- **Texts:** `head_goal` = "Ziel" (added to `_CHEAT_KEYS` in `planspiel/texts.py`); `bands_title` = "Bänder für den Schaden fürs Geschäft"; `intro` reworded: teams ask per asset and protection goal, the facilitator finds the case with the matching goal and answers as MechTech management with its band, never an amount, never an attack. Neither names the matrix. "Geschäftsführung" is kept.
- **Template (`print/templates/master.tex.j2`):** case table colspec `L | C{10mm} | L | p{38mm}`; header row prints case header, `goal_header`, then the remaining headers; each row prints `row.case`, bold `row.goal`, `row.answer`, `row.band`.
- **README:** the `spielleitung-uebersicht.pdf` bullet now reads "Antworten zu Geschäftsfolgen (Band und Schutzziel je Fall)"; "Hinweis-Matrix" (the clue matrix) stays.
- **Coverage rule:** `test_every_protection_goal_has_a_case` pins {c, i, a} all present, six to eight cases, at least one integrity case. Real config yields goals `C C I A A A A A` (8 cases).
